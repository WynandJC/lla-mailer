lla-mailer privacy policy

lla-mailer uses two Google permissions on its own account, rtiologsender@gmail.com, and on no other account:

  • Send email (gmail.send): to send log files and reminders to a fixed list of recipients.
  • Read email (gmail.readonly): to read replies to its own reminder emails. It cannot change or delete any email.
  • It does not collect, store or share data from any Google account. Nothing read is passed to anyone else.
  • It is used only by its owner. Nobody else signs in to it.

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: rtiologsender@gmail.com
