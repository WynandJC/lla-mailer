lla-mailer privacy policy

lla-mailer uses one Google permission: send email (gmail.send) on behalf of its own account, rtiologsender@gmail.com.

  • It cannot read, search, change or delete any email.
  • It does not collect, store or share any data from Google accounts.
  • It is used only by its owner. Nobody else signs in to it.

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: rtiologsender@gmail.com
